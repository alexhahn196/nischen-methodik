# 07 — 120-Minute Timeline Blueprint

Covers brief step 23. Values derive from `03_visual_analysis.md` and `05_editing_analysis.md`; where the blueprint goes beyond what was measured, the row says so.

**Target:** exactly 120 min · **~14,900 words** · **392 placed visual assets** · ~200 newly generated

> **Note on word count.** This blueprint is built for a hard 120:00 runtime, which at the SOP's 125 wpm comes to ~14,900 words. The SOP's 16,000-word target yields ~128 min — also inside the category band (competitors ship 119–137 min). Pick one and stay consistent: either write 14,900 words for a 120-min cut, or write 16,000 and let the episode run to ~128 min. **The second is closer to what Calm Science actually ships (131–134 min) and is the recommended default**; every per-section figure below then scales by ×1.07.

## Section notes

### 0:00–0:30 — Cold hook
Narration on frame one, no fade-in, no music. Three to four concrete, escalating instances of the phenomenon in the viewer's own world. Hard cuts every ~7 s. This is the brightest, densest, most stimulating half-minute in the video, and it is the only part you hand-edit. *(Measured: 17/17 videos start narration within 0.6 s; Gemini measured 6.4–7.5 s/asset in Sleep On Physics' opening.)*

### 0:30–3:00 — Paradox, question, CTA, first turn
The escalation resolves into a plain statement of the impossibility, then the explicit question — land it at **0:55–0:58**. Then the CTA at **0:58–1:16**, spoken over a near-static wide shot (this is the seam; Sleep On Physics literally cuts to a static starfield here). Music enters under it. From ~1:20 the spiral's first turn begins and the template takes over. *(Measured: first CTA at 47–87 s across 13 of 14 CTA-using videos.)*

### 3:00–10:00 — Context
Who discovered this, what they measured, what they got wrong. Still denser than the body (~5 changes/min, 12 s holds). The cut rate steps down to body level by 10:00 and **never comes back up**. *(Measured: 5.55 → 3.10 changes/min, a −44 % step completed by minute 10.)*

### 10:00–20:00 — The textbook answer
Peak information density. State the conventional explanation cleanly and completely, so that undermining it later has something to push against. First **hero cutaway** of the object goes here — the single most designed image in the video. First core-claim restatement lands around 18:00.

### 20:00–40:00 — The real mechanism
"But that isn't quite what happens." The field-theoretic / quantum account. This is where your cyan measurement geometry earns its place: cross-sections, scale bars, field lines. Restatement #2 near 34:00.

### 40:00–60:00 — Analogy and its limits
One strong analogy, developed for several minutes, then explicitly broken: "but the analogy fails here, and the way it fails is the interesting part." *(Measured: 12–18 analogy markers/hour on the strongest script.)* Restatement #3 near 55:00.

### 60:00–90:00 — Deepest layer
The longest section and the least eventful. Consequences, edge cases, what follows if the mechanism is true. Cut rate flat at 3.0/min, images increasingly abstract. Restatements #4 and #5 near 68:00 and 84:00. **Do not introduce a new hook here.** No channel in the sample re-escalates after minute 10.

### 90:00–110:00 — Cosmic escalation
The same physics, at cosmic scale. This is where the 40 % cosmic-physics content mix pays off inside a fundamental-physics episode: the electron becomes stellar plasma, the wire becomes a magnetar's field. Information density drops; the language does the work. Restatement #6 near 104:00.

### 110:00–120:00 — Close
Open questions — what nobody knows. Final restatement in the plainest possible words. Optional soft CTA at ~118:30. Narration continues to roughly **119:20**, then a 4-second fade to black. No end card, no outro animation, no next-video pitch. *(Measured: no end cards observed; final frames near-black on every channel.)*

## What this blueprint deliberately does NOT do

- **No chapters, no on-screen section markers.** The 9 movements are invisible to the viewer. *(0 chapter markers in 17/17 videos.)*
- **No mid-video re-escalation.** Visual intensity is monotonically non-increasing after minute 10.
- **No mid-roll CTA.** One at 60 s, optionally one at the end.
- **No brightness ramp-up.** Brightness only ever falls: 42 → 26.
- **No music swell.** The bed enters once and thins out; it never builds.
